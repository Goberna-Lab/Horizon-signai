    Own point

    Anywhere within 190 km of the south pole (down to ~83.7° S). The horizon comes from the 240 m LOLA terrain and is approximate: at that resolution terrain smaller than ~500 m does not appear, and on ridges and crater rims it can differ a lot from the RoI horizons. To decide, use an RoI from the catalogue (SPEC E24).

    Landing scenario
